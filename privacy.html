Privacy Policy for rclone-drive

Last updated: 2026-11-24

rclone-drive is a personal application used solely by its owner. It connects the open-source tool rclone to the owner's own Google Drive account through the Google Drive API.

Data accessed

The application accesses only files in the owner's own Google Drive, and only to perform operations the owner initiates: uploading backups of his personal servers, syncing his private file library, and reading or deleting those same files.

Data collection and sharing

  • No data is collected from any third party.
  • No data is sold, shared, or disclosed to any third party.
  • No data is used for advertising or analytics.
  • All transferred data belongs to the owner and resides in the owner's own Google Drive. The owner can delete it at any time.

Contact

The owner can be reached via the support email shown on the application's OAuth consent screen.
